Multicam Timecode Sync Recorder beta — guide for testers

Thank you for testing. This guide takes ten minutes to read and saves hours of guessing.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- one or more Blackmagic DeckLink cards with Blackmagic Desktop Video installed
- an ATEM switcher on the same network, with its aux outputs cabled to the DeckLink inputs over SDI.
  The ATEM embeds its timecode in every SDI output: that is what lines the files up.
- DaVinci Resolve Studio for the ready-made project. Without it you still get FCPXML, Premiere XML and EDL
- a fast drive, preferably NVMe: at 25 fps each DNxHD 185 track writes about 23 MB/s, twice as much
  at 50 and 60 fps

FORMATS
The recorder reads the format and frame rate from the ATEM and records exactly that: no scaling, no
frame rate conversion. It follows the ATEM by itself when the standard changes, while it is not recording.
- 1080p at 23.98, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 50, 59.94 and 60
- 1080i at 50, 59.94 and 60
- 2160p, EXPERIMENTAL, at the same eight progressive rates
DNxHD in HD, high, medium or low quality; its name follows the frame rate (185 at 25p, 220 at 29.97p,
365 at 50p...). DNxHR HQ, SQ or LB in UHD. At 29.97 and 59.94 the timecode is drop-frame when the ATEM
sends it so. An input that receives a different format from the ATEM stays empty and says so in red.
UHD needs 12G-SDI or HDMI 2.0 inputs (3G cards such as the DeckLink Duo 2 cannot receive it) and about
four times the CPU and drive of HD. It has been recorded and turned into a Resolve project, but never
yet with a 4K ATEM: if you have one, your test is the most wanted.

INSTALLING
Download "mtsrec-<version>.msi" from https://github.com/angeliandrea/mtsrec/releases and run it.
The installer is not signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
then "Run anyway". On Windows 11 with Smart App Control turned on it is blocked altogether, with error
4551 (Windows Security > App & browser control shows whether it is on): install on a computer where it
is off, or wait for the signed version. It installs for your user only and needs no administrator rights.
Had WinISORecorder, the name of this program up to 0.9 beta 5? Just install: it removes the old version
and keeps your settings, setups and input map.

FIRST LAUNCH
1. The system check runs once, about twenty seconds: CPU, memory, cards and PCIe links, Desktop Video,
   drives, network, Windows settings and an encoding test. It tells you how many tracks this machine can
   record and what to fix. Fix the red items before recording.
2. "Open recorder". Off air, press "Map inputs..." under the tracks: it finds which ATEM aux output is
   cabled to each DeckLink input, switching each aux for a moment and putting it back. Then add tracks
   with "+ Add track", or "Add all inputs" for every DeckLink input of the computer, mapped inputs first
   in aux order. "Tracks possible" shows the limit and what sets it.
3. Choose the destination, set the ATEM address if it was not found by itself, and save the whole
   configuration with "Save as…" if you want to recall it.

WHAT TO TEST, IN THIS ORDER
1. A 1-minute recording with a few cuts and dissolves on the program, and two markers.
   At STOP check: no dropped frames, the Resolve project opens with the multicam and the cut timeline.
2. Ten minutes with all the tracks your machine handles.
3. One hour, if you can: this is the test that says the most.
4. Anything your control room does differently: other ATEM models, HDMI inputs, converters,
   Thunderbolt boxes, a HyperDeck recording alongside ("HyperDeck clips…").
5. The frame rate you work at, if it is not 25: above all 29.97 and 59.94 with drop-frame timecode, and
   1080i. Check that the cuts in Resolve land on the right frame.
6. With a 4K ATEM: the same tests in 2160p. Check that the timecode is "TC FROM SIGNAL" on every track
   and that the cuts in Resolve land on the right frame.
Always off air, or with another recorder running: this is a beta.

SENDING THE RESULTS
After each test press "Test report…" (top bar of the recorder, it lights up after STOP).
- You see everything that is inside before anything is created.
- Write a few notes: ATEM model, cards, cabling, what you tested, what went wrong.
- "Create report and email it" saves a small zip (about 10 KB), opens its folder and prepares an email
  to info@angeliandrea.it. Attach the zip and send. If no mail program opens, send it from your webmail
  with the subject shown on screen.

The report contains no video, no IP addresses, no computer or user names, no file paths and no recording
or source names: they are replaced with the DeckLink port names. A random ID, chosen once per machine,
tells the reports apart. Nothing is ever sent without you.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Note what you did, then send a test report anyway, with your notes: a failed test is the most useful one.
A recording interrupted by a crash or power loss is repaired automatically the next time the recorder
opens.

THE USER MANUAL
The full manual is a PDF: the Help button in the recorder, F1, or "Multicam Timecode Sync Recorder - user manual" in the
Start menu. If the recorder finds that Desktop Video, DaVinci Resolve Studio or WebView2 is missing, it
shows a window with the link to the official download page.
