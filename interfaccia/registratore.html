Tracks: + Add track under the list, up to what this machine handles; × on the track number removes it. R: the track is recorded. Setup saves settings and tracks under a name. The file name is the name of the source on the aux, read from the ATEM: type over it to change it, before REC. Levels in peak dBFS on the ATEM mixer scale: green up to −20, yellow up to −9, then red. Audio is recorded as is, with no processing. Thumbnails refresh in turn, never at the expense of recording. Markers: the button, Enter in the text field or M on the keyboard; from the console, Enter. Alt + mouse wheel on the timeline: horizontal zoom around the playhead, or around the mouse pointer if nothing has been recorded yet. Shift + mouse wheel: scrolls the timeline. Double-click on the ruler or Ctrl+Z: back to the full view. The bar at the bottom scrolls the timeline.

Test report you see everything that is inside, and you decide whether to send it

A small zip of text files: the system check of this machine, the settings and the last five recordings, with frames written and dropped per track, timecode, CPU load, cuts, markers and the Resolve project. No video, no IP addresses, no computer or user names, no file paths, no recording or source names.

Notes for the developer, optional: switcher model, cabling, what you tested, what went wrong.
